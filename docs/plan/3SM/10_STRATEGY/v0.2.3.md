# v0.2.3 — Pool-estimate triangulation (how many products are on the EU market?)

## Abstract

The v0.2.2 funnel delivered Q1 = [85,840–343,360] (modeled), Q2 =
17,170 (counted floor), Q3 = 3,590 (modeled reach). User direction
(2026-09-14): the Q1 range is **too wide and its basis opaque**; the
user's own prior is 20k–50k total products. Unit v0.2.3 is
**research-intensive, minimal development**: triangulate

- **(a) the auditable-products pool** — unique identified products
  with substantial data (several product properties) plus a reachable
  SDS — and
- **(b) the total distinct paint products on the EU market** — the
  crux,

from independent anchors, and re-derive the funnel with an argued,
narrower range. No new scraping (D31); development limited to
analysis queries over the staging DB and a report section.

## Research findings (2026-09-14 evidence pass)

The pass ran as three parallel research briefs (ECAT authority;
register landscape; literature). All sources URL- and access-dated
(2026-09-14); the briefs are the project's external-knowledge record.

1. **ECAT is authoritative but partial — and certified-subset-only.**
   It is *the* legal EU Ecolabel register (Art. 9(10) Reg. (EC)
   66/2010: competent bodies notify awards, the Commission maintains
   the public register; monthly bulk export, publisher DG ENV). But
   registration is licence-holder self-service — the Commission:
   *"This database may therefore be a non-exhaustive list"* — and the
   EUEB status of 18 April 2024 (quoted by BEUC) records **87% of
   licences and 69% of products in ECAT, with EANs missing in many
   cases** (independently corroborates our measured 16% identity
   completeness). Our export (88,920 rows) vs the Commission's awarded
   count (116,692 products, 03/2026) ≈ 76% — consistent.
2. **RECONCILIATION FLAG:** the Commission counts **38,233 paints &
   varnishes** (facts & figures 03/2026; 37,000 03/2024 → 38,096
   09/2025) vs **our staged 17,838** (group-044 filter) — factor
   ≈2.1. Hypotheses: our filter narrower than the Commission's
   category; the awarded-vs-registered gap; and ECAT "products"
   including **shade/packaging variants of one base formulation**
   (JRC AHWG 2024) — exactly our D2 formulation-unit collapse. Must
   be resolved: it scales Q2 and the ppp factor directly.
3. **No ecolabel market share is published.** SWD(2017) 253: market-
   share data *"are not collected as part of the monitoring
   obligations of the scheme."* The only official penetration figure
   found anywhere: JRC 2012 — ≈30% of UK **trade-sector** paints
   Ecolabelled (dated, sectoral, one country).
4. **No published EU paint product count exists** — associations
   (CEPE: companies/turnover only), trade press, market research
   (values/volumes only) and academia all skip it. A **documented
   gap the study fills**.
5. **Assortment sizes are long-tailed:** Tikkurila ≈190 products
   (countable own catalogue); Brillux >12,000 articles (full-range
   supplier incl. tools); OBI ≈4,900 lacquer items online; BAUHAUS
   AT 549 products / 806 variants. The uniform **ppp = 107.3 is the
   pool model's core weakness**.
6. **All-product registers point high:** Sweden (KemI Products
   Register, broadest scope in the EU — *all* chemical products
   ≥100 kg/a, hazardous or not): ~100,000 products/yr, >200,000
   cumulative; a 2022 BfR slide lists **decorative paints 36,843 /
   protective & functional 23,822 / other coatings 10,566**
   (register-products-vs-PCN-dossiers ambiguity — extraction task).
   Denmark: ~38–40k notified products + an **open CC0 dataset with
   counts by function** (paint counts extractable; framework
   notifications collapse shade series — downward paint bias).
   Norway: ~40k declared (2009; paints coverage measured 82%).
   **PCN:** 1.4M mixtures notified EU-wide 2021 (SWD(2022) 435);
   3.94M submissions 2019–22 incl. updates; hazardous-only; ~19%
   non-compliance (Forum pilot 2025); per-product DB not public.
   Germany/France/UK: no public product registers. SPIN: substance-
   level only (confirms D33) — not a product-count source.
7. **Producer counts:** CEPE ~800 members (85% of market value);
   VdL ~200 (>90% DE); BCF 250 (95% UK); Fipec 150 (95% FR);
   Prodcom France 245 legal units; SBS NACE 20.30 = 3,200 (incl.
   inks + mastics — verify directly in Eurostat).
8. **Volume context:** EU consumption ≈9.1 Mt/yr (Ceresana);
   EU27 paints sold 3.7–4.2 Mt/yr (JRC AHWG 2024).

**Preliminary synthesis.** The 20k–50k prior is **unsupported**: the
ECAT certified minority alone holds 17.8k–38.2k paint products, and a
50k total would imply >35–75% ecolabel penetration of the market —
implausible for a voluntary label (even the 2012 UK 30% trade-sector
figure reads ≈130k total from the current ECAT count). The all-product
registers agree (Sweden's paints plausibly 10k+ for 10M people; PCN's
1.4M mixtures with paints plausibly 10–20% → 140k–280k hazardous
ones). The one honest compression is the **formulation vs SKU unit**
(D2 variant collapse, plausibly 3–10×). Working hypothesis for (b):
**≈80k–300k at registration level, ≈40k–150k at formulation level** —
to be resolved by the anchors, not asserted. For (a), the user's
3k–17k bracket (Q3 reach to Q2 floor) stands as the outer envelope.

## Method — four independent anchors

- **A1 — ECAT-inverse:** certified count ÷ penetration denominator
  (literature; JRC145238 §3.4.3 extraction; the 2012 UK 30% as a
  dated upper bound).
- **A2 — national-register scaling:** SE/DK/NO paint-category counts
  × EU scaling with scope corrections (≥100 kg/a thresholds,
  framework notifications, measured coverage 75–82%).
- **A3 — PCN mixture share:** paint share of the 1.4M notified
  mixtures (Swedish/Danish category breakdowns) + an uplift for
  non-hazardous (non-notified) decorative paints.
- **A4 — long-tail bottom-up:** producer-size × assortment
  distributions, calibrated with **our own staged PE
  `products_listed` distribution** plus the Tikkurila/Brillux
  anchors — replacing the uniform ppp; per-CN8 weighting kept from
  v0.2.2 (s(cn8)).

Deliverable (a) operationalizes **auditable** = distinct
(manufacturer, product-ident) with ≥N product properties **and** an
SDS reachable — a tiered count from the staging DB + PE data, not an
assertion.

## Design-turn reframe (2026-09-14; amends this strategy)

The design turn (`20_DESIGN/units/v0.2.3.md`, CEO review HOLD SCOPE)
reframed the unit on user direction:

- **Meta-benchmarking over reconciliation** — the deliverable is a
  **magnitude-class verdict** (contiguous classes: 20k–50k / 50k–
  100k / 100k–200k / 200k–300k / >300k), **dual-level** (SKU +
  formulation), under a pinned confidence rule; the four anchors
  became six benchmarks **B1–B6** (tonnage-Fermi, register scaling,
  PCN share, bottom-up, ECAT-inverse + empirical ECAT∩DK penetration
  probe, literature sweep) in a class-vote table.
- **T4 amended:** the ECAT 17,838-vs-38,233 reconciliation is a
  supporting input to B5 and a consistency column — **no publish
  gate**.
- **Danish staging added** (user decision): the AT CC0 dataset is
  staged as real rows (one adapter; aggregates-only fallback to
  extraction) — a scope addition beyond the "out" clause below; D3
  official-export discipline intact.
- **Supersession:** Q1 v2 replaces Q1 v0 as the live-report
  headline; v1 stays marked superseded with snapshot references.

  **argued range**, never a point estimate.

  for the final range (see the design-turn reframe above).
