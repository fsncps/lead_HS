# Topic — Lead & SDS (strategy summary)

## Abstract

Lead enters paints as pigments (lead chromate/molybdate, red lead,
lead white), anticorrosives and driers (octoates/naphthenates); the
detection instrument is a lead-compound dictionary (CAS/EC/Index,
v0.1 scope) applied to SDS Section 3, with UFI as join key and
Section 15 statements as anomaly signal. The EU legal frame (Annex
XVII 16/17; 17 Mar 2022 authorisation refusal) and the Swiss layer
(ChemRRV Anhang 2.8 ≥ 0.01% total Pb, shielded through the VIPaV CdD
exception) frame the interpretation. Verified EU-market lead-white/
red-lead products document the low-frequency tails. Feasibility:
SDS obtainability varies strongly by channel — see the data-source
topic.

## DECISIONS

- D5 — lead detection primary source: SDS Section 3 vs CAS/EC/Index
  dictionary; UFI join; Section 15 anomaly signal.
- D15 — headline caveat: the 100–1000 ppm band is invisible to the SDS
  method (Swiss ban below the EU declaration floor); carried in every
  deliverable.
- D27 — no legal-status fields in the dictionary or database; the EU/Swiss
  legal lens lives in the study documentation.
- D12 — EU-lawful presence of lead paints is a first-class study object
  (seed products on file: WERDOL Bleimennige, BRAVA blymönja, Old Holland
  PW1, Zecchi).
