# Topic — Methodology (strategy summary)

## Abstract

How the study measures the EU paint market with documents only, at
minimal cost, with no laboratory. Unit of analysis is the **base
formulation** (SKU counting rejected); the population (10⁴–10⁵
formulations, estimate) must be **triangulated** because no official
register counts paint products; precision-based **stratified
sampling** (~2,000–3,000 products by 95% margins; strata by segment ×
origin) draws from a de-duplicated catalogue frame; lead is detected
by **SDS Section 3 parsing** against a lead-compound dictionary, and
suspected findings are **corroborated across independent documents**
instead of being lab-tested. Full detail and rationale: public
methodology document (link below).

## DECISIONS

- D2 — unit of analysis: base formulation (colour/size variants collapse).
- D4 — precision-based stratified sampling, n ≈ 385/stratum (±5%),
  ≈ 811 (±1.5%), FPC for small frames.
- D7 — corroboration instead of laboratory (hard constraint).
- D13 — HS 3213 artists' colours census annex (demoted to low-priority
  annex by D31).
- D15 — headline caveat: Swiss ban 100 ppm sits below the EU SDS
  declaration floor 0.1%; the 100–1000 ppm band is method-invisible.
- D27 — product data only in tool/database/reports; the legal lens
  stays in the study documentation.
- D30/D31 — the deliverable emphasis is numbers-first access coverage
  (N2/N3), reconnaissance-only, EU-only.
- Frame-decision bridge: catalogue strata are created only by recorded
  manual promotion from verified census floors (D20).
